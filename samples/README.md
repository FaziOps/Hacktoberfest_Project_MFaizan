# Sample photos

Put fabric photos for the demo here (`.jpg`, `.png` or `.webp`). Good demo set:

- 1 clean piece of fabric (should come back with no defects)
- 1 oil or tea stain
- 1 small hole or cut
- 1 pulled or broken thread
- 1 crease or colour patch

Take them in good light, with the camera about 30 cm above the fabric, so the photo shows about 30 cm
across (the app's default "Photo shows" setting).
